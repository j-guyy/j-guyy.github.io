    Metro areas are the cities of the UN's World Urbanization Prospects 2025, with 2025 populations. Each one is the whole continuous built-up area, drawn the same way in every country from satellite population grids (1 km² cells of 1,500+ people per km²) rather than by city limits. That's why Jakarta (41.9 million) and Dhaka (36.6 million) now rank above Tokyo (33.4 million). Other rankings, like Demographia's, still put Tokyo first, and official definitions such as US metropolitan statistical areas (MSAs), which add commuter counties, often come out larger.
